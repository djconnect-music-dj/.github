# DJConnect

**Play your music. DJConnect brings it to life.**

DJConnect is an AI DJ for Home Assistant-powered music experiences. It brings music, storytelling and interaction together in a DJ Session across devices in your home.

[Explore DJConnect](https://djconnect.dev)
